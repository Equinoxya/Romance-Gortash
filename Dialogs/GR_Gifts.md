# Extension - Gifts

Small items left at camp between scenes. Notation is explained in the README.

## How it works

- Each gift is added to the player's inventory at the first long rest after its trigger (`GR_Letters.txt`).
- The note is part of the item description, so no readable book is needed.
- These are plain items with no stats. Turning the knife or the key into usable gear is optional.

| Gift | Trigger | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Bottle | `GR_Romance_Interested` | arrives with the ball invitation |
| Dance card | `GR_Ball_Danced` | |
| Key | `GR_Romance_Committed` | |
| Knife | `GR_Romance_Committed` | Dark Urge only |

## Bottle

**GIFT1_NAME - Item name**
- EN: Bottle of Tethyrian Red
- FR: Bouteille de rouge du Téthyr

**GIFT1_DESC - Item description**
- EN: Older than his ambitions, though not by much. The tag at its neck reads: "You left before the bottle was finished. I dislike unfinished business. Drink it with whomever you like, but think of the man who paid for it. - E.G."
- FR: Plus vieux que ses ambitions, mais de peu. Sur l'étiquette nouée au goulot : « Vous avez quitté mon bureau avant la fin de la bouteille. J'ai horreur des affaires inachevées. Buvez-la avec qui vous voudrez, mais pensez à celui qui l'a payée. - E.G. »
